## What is TCP/IP?

TCP/IP (Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol) is the standard set of communication protocols used for transmitting data across networks, including the internet. It allows computers and other devices to communicate reliably by breaking data into packets and ensuring those packets reach the correct destination.

TCP is responsible for delivering data accurately and in the correct order, while IP handles addressing and routing the packets between devices.

## Key Components

- Transmission Control Protocol (TCP)
- Internet Protocol (IP)
- IP Addresses
- Ports
- Packets

## Why is TCP/IP Important?

Nearly every modern network relies on TCP/IP. Understanding these protocols is essential for troubleshooting network issues, configuring devices, and maintaining secure communications.

> TCP/IP is the foundation of communication on today's networks.
