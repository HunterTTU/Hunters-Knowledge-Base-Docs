## What is Multi-Factor Authentication?

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) adds an extra layer of security by requiring users to provide two or more forms of verification before accessing an account. Common authentication factors include something you know, something you have, and something you are.

MFA significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access caused by stolen passwords.

## Common Authentication Factors

- Passwords
- Authentication apps
- Security keys
- Fingerprints
- Facial recognition

## Why is MFA Important?

Even if an attacker steals a password, MFA makes it much more difficult to access an account without the additional verification method.

> Multi-Factor Authentication is one of the most effective ways to protect online accounts.
