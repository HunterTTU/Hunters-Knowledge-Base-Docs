## What are Cloud Service Models?

Cloud service models describe the different ways cloud computing services are delivered to users. The three primary models are Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Software as a Service (SaaS). Each model provides different levels of control, flexibility, and management responsibilities.

Organizations choose a service model based on their technical needs, budget, and desired level of control.

## Common Service Models

- Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)
- Platform as a Service (PaaS)
- Software as a Service (SaaS)

## Why are Cloud Service Models Important?

Understanding cloud service models helps businesses choose the right solution for hosting applications, storing data, and managing IT resources while balancing cost and security.

> Choosing the right cloud service model can improve efficiency and reduce infrastructure costs.
