## What is GDPR?

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a privacy law created by the European Union to protect personal data and give individuals more control over how organizations collect and use their information.

Organizations that process data from EU residents must comply with GDPR requirements.

## Key Principles

- Transparency
- Data minimization
- Lawful processing
- Data security
- Accountability

## Why is GDPR Important?

GDPR establishes strict privacy standards and encourages organizations to handle personal information responsibly.
