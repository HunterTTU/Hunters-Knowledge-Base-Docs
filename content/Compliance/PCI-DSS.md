## What is PCI DSS?

The Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) is a set of security requirements designed to protect credit card information during payment processing.

Organizations that accept payment cards must follow these standards to reduce fraud and protect customer information.

## Requirements

- Secure networks
- Encrypt cardholder data
- Monitor systems
- Restrict access
- Perform regular security testing

## Why is PCI DSS Important?

PCI DSS helps businesses protect financial information while reducing the risk of payment card fraud.
