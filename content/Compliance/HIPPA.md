## What is HIPAA?

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is a United States law designed to protect sensitive patient health information. Healthcare providers, insurance companies, and other covered organizations must follow strict security and privacy requirements.

## Security Requirements

- Protect electronic health records
- Limit data access
- Encrypt sensitive information
- Train employees
- Monitor systems

## Why is HIPAA Important?

HIPAA helps protect patient privacy while ensuring healthcare organizations maintain strong security controls.
