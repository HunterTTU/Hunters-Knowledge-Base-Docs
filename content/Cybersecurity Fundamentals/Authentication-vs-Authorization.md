title: Authentication vs Authorization
date: 2026-07-23
---


## Authentication

Authentication verifies a user's identity. Examples include passwords, fingerprints, and security keys.

## Authorization

Authorization determines what an authenticated user is allowed to access.

## Why is the Difference Important?

Understanding the difference helps organizations implement stronger access controls and reduce unauthorized access.
