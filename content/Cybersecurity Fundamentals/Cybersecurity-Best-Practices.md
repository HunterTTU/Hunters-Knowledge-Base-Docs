## What are Cybersecurity Best Practices?

Cybersecurity best practices are recommended actions that help individuals and organizations reduce the risk of cyberattacks.

## Best Practices

- Use strong passwords
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication
- Keep software updated
- Back up important data
- Avoid suspicious emails
- Use secure Wi-Fi
- Install antivirus software

## Why are Best Practices Important?

Following basic cybersecurity practices significantly reduces the likelihood of malware infections, phishing attacks, and data breaches.

> Good cybersecurity habits are the first line of defense against cyber threats.
