## What is the CIA Triad?

The CIA Triad is a cybersecurity model based on three core principles: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability. These principles guide organizations when designing secure systems.

## Components

- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability

## Why is it Important?

The CIA Triad provides a simple framework for evaluating the effectiveness of cybersecurity controls.
